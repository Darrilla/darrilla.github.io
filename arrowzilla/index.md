## A little logic. A little discovery.

An arrow can leave only when its exit is clear. Another arrow's tail may be
in the way, so the order matters. Open a path, clear a few more arrows, and
watch the board change.

ArrowZilla mixes grid puzzles with picture levels. The picture reveal is the
reward along the way: animals, objects, and places emerge as you solve.

## Take a closer look

- **Tap to release** an arrow with a clear exit.
- **Hold near a head** to preview its exit path: green for clear, red for blocked.
- **Pinch to zoom** and use two fingers to pan around a busy board.
- **Use Zap** to send all currently clear arrows away together.
- **Replay for stars** and try to finish with fewer blocked attempts.

Progress and completed-level ratings are saved on your device.
