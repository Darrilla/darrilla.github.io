Last updated: October 8, 2026

ArrowZilla is an arrow puzzle game made by Darrilla Software. This policy
describes the current development prototype.

## Information the game collects

The current prototype does not ask for a name, email address, location,
contacts, or an account. Darrilla Software does not collect personal
information through the game.

## Progress on your device

The game stores the selected level, unlocked levels, completion records, and
last and best star ratings locally on your device. These records support
resuming and replaying your puzzles. They are not sent to a Darrilla server,
and the prototype does not provide account-based cloud sync.

Removing the app or clearing its data can remove this local progress.

## Analytics, ads, and purchases

The current prototype has no active remote analytics, advertising SDK,
or in-app purchase integration. Unity's connected analytics, advertising,
and cloud diagnostic services are disabled in the current project.

## Support messages

If you email Darrilla Software, we receive your email address and the
information you choose to send, such as a device model, screenshot, or bug
description. We use it to answer your request and investigate the issue.
Please do not send passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information.

## Sharing and children's privacy

Darrilla Software does not sell game data or share it for advertising. The
current prototype does not knowingly collect personal information from children
through gameplay.

## Future versions

If a future release adds services that change how information is handled,
this page will be updated to describe them and show a revised date.

## Contact

For privacy questions or requests about information you've sent in a support
email, contact [darrilla@gmail.com](mailto:darrilla@gmail.com).
