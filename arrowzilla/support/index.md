ArrowZilla is in development. This page covers the current prototype.

## Contact Darrilla

Email [darrilla@gmail.com](mailto:darrilla@gmail.com?subject=ArrowZilla%20support)
with questions, bug reports, or feedback.

For an issue, include your device model, operating system and build version,
the level number or picture name, and what happened. A screenshot or short
recording helps us understand arrow selection and reveal problems. Leave out
private information.

## Why won't an arrow leave?

Another arrow's tail may cross its exit path. Clear the blocker first. Hold
near the arrow's head to see its exit ray: green means clear; red means blocked.
Hold it again to hide that ray.

## How do I inspect a crowded board?

Pinch to zoom and drag with two fingers to pan. Then tap the arrow you want
to release. Holding near a head previews its path without releasing it.

## What does Zap do?

**Zap** releases every arrow that is currently clear in one batch. It can
uncover the next set of available moves. **Super Zap** repeats the process
while arrows can leave.

## How do stars work?

A completed level earns three stars with no blocked attempts. A small number
of blocked attempts earns two; more earns one. The game saves your last and
best completed ratings. Restart a level to try for a cleaner run.

## Where is my progress saved?

The current prototype saves your selected level, unlocked levels, and ratings
on your device. It does not provide account-based cloud sync. Clearing app data
or removing the app can remove that progress.

## Where can I download it?

ArrowZilla is still in development. Public store links will be added to the
[ArrowZilla overview](/arrowzilla/) when available.
